# MGB NSI Launchpad

**Mass General Brigham Neuroscience Institute (NSI) Incubator-Accelerator**

Live Website: [https://pnhadar.github.io/NSIL/](https://pnhadar.github.io/NSIL/)

An NSI-based commercialization pathway (in close coordination with **MGB Innovation**) that helps promising clinical and scientific neuroscience discoveries across Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH), Brigham and Women's Hospital (BWH), and Harvard Medical School (HMS) become viable, investor-ready ventures.

## Program Architecture

- **Stage 1 Incubator**: 3–5 teams in the inaugural cohort • ~12 weeks (weekly structured curriculum, Mentor Network access, company-formation guidance, Demo Day, and non-dilutive team prize). Open to any MGB employee with a plausible neuroscience company idea; no prior IP, prototype, or existing company required.
- **Stage 2 Accelerator**: Up to 3 ventures per cycle • ~6 months of individualized venture support, strategic introductions, Investor Pitch Day, and SAFE-based investment.
